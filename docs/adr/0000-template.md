# NNNN. Title in the imperative or as a short noun phrase

- Status: Proposed | Accepted | Deprecated | Superseded by NNNN
- Date: YYYY-MM-DD
- Milestone: Mx

## Context

What problem are we solving? What forces are at play (requirements,
constraints, what the docs say)? State facts, not the solution.

## Decision

What we will do, in one or two short paragraphs. Use active voice:
"We will ...".

## Consequences

What becomes easier, what becomes harder, and what we now have to build or
watch. Include the negative consequences.

## Alternatives considered

- **Option name:** one or two sentences, and why we did not choose it.
